Why this catalogue exists

Choose with curiosity, not hype.

NEXUS is a small design exercise for browsing and comparing game concepts. It presents no storefront, account system, downloads, or live community features.

  1. 01

    Fiction first

    Every game is invented for this demo. Names, descriptions, release years, and platform labels do not describe products you can buy or download.

  2. 02

    Editorial signals

    Scores and badges are made-up presentation cues. They support comparison and emphasis, not real reviews, rankings, or recommendations.

  3. 03

    Local by default

    The catalogue ships with the page and search runs in your browser. There is no sign-in, analytics, tracking, or network request behind the experience.
